DSiTube help

On DSiTube, you can watch videos on your DSi. Just choose a video and wait untill the video is loaded.

Sometimes, it happens that your video does not load, or the video stops playing, or you get a message saying "out of memory". This sometimes happens because your DSi has no memory left.

This is what you should do:
- Click the button on the bottom of your screen.
- Click on "history"
- Choose "delete history".
- Click the button (on the bottom of your screen).

If this does not work, try turning off and on your DSi, and select your disired video again.

Have fun on DSiTube!
